    ⚠ One trophy can be missed

    Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots. One spot is at Jason's house and is only open in Chapter 1. Meditate there before Chapter 1 ends, or the trophy may be locked for the playthrough. Everything else can be earned after the story. See the Missables tab for the full watch list.

    Roadmap to the platinum

    Five stages, in order — about 200 hours in total (PowerPyx, KeenGamer). Story first, then strangers, then activities, then collectibles, then the crime and money cleanup — and the 100% completion trophy last. Stages overlap: the 100% checklist fills during stages 2–4.

          Sources & honesty

          Official names, descriptions and grades are as PlayStation publishes them (Exophase and PSNProfiles trophy lists, 18 November 2026). Each guide step names its sources beside it. The game released 19 November 2026 and guides are still confirming a few unlocks: where a method is unconfirmed, the guide says so instead of guessing — a cited, honest guide beats a detailed invented one. No guide text here is copied from another guide.

          Hidden trophies: 28 of the 55 stay hidden on PlayStation until earned (12 story trophies and 16 side ones). This guide marks them HIDDEN. Turn on spoiler-free mode in the Tracker to hide story trophy names and descriptions until you tick them earned.

          All 55 trophies are offline and single-player. There are no online trophies. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave cheats off for a trophy run (reported by GTABase and PowerPyx).
